# Off-page SEO (backlinks) — the safe way

Off-page SEO = what the rest of the internet says about you. It can help, but it is also how most sites get destroyed
after a Google algorithm update. **Great keywords + great content come first.** Do this only on top.

## ❌ Never
- "100 backlinks for $5" offers.
- PBNs (Private Blog Networks) / link farms: one person owns thousands of sites linking to you. Google detects them,
  and the penalty can be permanent.
- Any "SEO expert" who can't show you a site they worked on, with the start date and the traffic since.

## ✅ The 4 legit tactics
1. **Broken link building.** In Semrush, audit a big site in your niche, find their outbound links that return 404.
   Ask Claude to build a page covering the same topic, then email the site: "Your link to X is broken, here is a page that covers it."
2. **Guest posting.** Search Google for `"<your niche>" "write for us"` (with the quotes) — e.g. `"plumbing" "write for us"`
   or `"سباكة" "اكتب معنا"`. Write a genuinely good article for them with one link back to your site.
3. **Journalist requests (digital PR).** Platforms like Qwoted, Featured or Help a B2B Writer post questions from journalists.
   Answer as the expert; they cite you with a link.
4. **Paid placements on reputable sites.** Real publications/blogs in your niche that sell sponsored posts. Few, relevant, high quality.

Also free and safe: Google Business Profile, real directories for your country/industry, local chamber of commerce,
suppliers and partners you work with.
