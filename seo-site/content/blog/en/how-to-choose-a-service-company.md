Wondering **how to choose a service company** you won't regret? Most of us pick the same way: first name on Google, cheapest price, or "my cousin used them once". Then we spend three weeks chasing them on WhatsApp, watching their "last seen" like a suspense series.

Good news: seven simple questions are enough to spot the right company from day one. Curious how we'd answer them? See [our services](/en/services/).

## 1. Is the company officially registered?

The first and easiest question. Ask for their business registration number and check it yourself with your country's official registry (in Saudi Arabia, the [Ministry of Commerce lookup](https://mc.gov.sa/ar/eservices/Pages/Commercial-data.aspx)). A serious company hands it over without blinking. If they dodge, that's your answer.

## 2. What do past clients say? (Reviews)

Read the Google Maps reviews, not just the stars. Focus on:

- **Negative reviews:** what went wrong, and how did the company respond? A respectful reply to a bad review can reassure you more than twenty perfect ones.
- **Dates:** recent reviews matter more than five-year-old ones.
- **Details:** "great!" tells you nothing. "Delivered early and cleaned up after" tells you a lot.

## 3. Is the quote written and detailed?

A written quote needs three things: **exactly what they'll do, what it costs, and when it'll be done**. Anything verbal basically didn't happen.

> Golden rule: get three quotes. The one that's far cheaper than the other two usually leaves something out, and you'll pay the difference later.

## 4. Do they explain things in plain language?

A company hiding behind jargon either doesn't understand its own work or wants you to feel like you don't. The right one walks you through your options and tells you honestly when you don't need something.

## 5. What happens after delivery?

Ask about the guarantee and follow-up: how long, what it covers, and who you call if something goes wrong. A clear answer here separates a company that wants a long-term client from one that wants a single sale.

## 6. Red flags when hiring: when to walk away

Walk away if they:

- Pressure you to decide today "because the offer ends tonight".
- Ask for full payment upfront with no contract.
- Have no address or landline.
- Reply slowly before you sign (expect slower after).

For more on common tricks, the US FTC's [guide to avoiding contractor scams](https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/how-avoid-home-improvement-scam) is useful in any industry.

## 7. Do you feel comfortable with them?

This one doesn't fit in a spreadsheet, but it matters. If you feel heard and your time is respected, your gut is usually right.

## The bottom line

Choosing a reliable service company isn't luck. It's seven questions: official registration, real reviews, a written quote, plain explanations, a guarantee, zero pressure, and comfort.
