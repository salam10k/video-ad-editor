# /voice

1. Ask for 3–5 texts the owner wrote (any language) and optionally 1–2 passages by others whose style they love ("humor reference").
2. Update `projects/<p>/brand.md` (merge, never wipe) under: Voice (tone, sentence length, dialect, form of address, recurring phrases) ·
   Humor (the kind of jokes, 2–3 quoted examples, limits) · Opinions · Confirmed facts / real numbers (only numbers the owner actually stated) · Stories.
3. The video's humor rule goes in by default unless the owner objects: "If the first 50 words don't land a joke, a wink or a relatable scene, rewrite them."
4. Show a before/after of the opening of the latest draft in `content/` so the owner can approve the voice.
