# Brand rules — MalFun

Source: the owner's brand-voice rules (malfun-brand-voice v2.0.0), copied 2026-10-05.

## Voice
- German, **"du"** form by default. English only on request.
- Warm, playful, family-focused: painting together, screen-free time, gifts.
- Hashtags: #malfun #meinmalfun

## Product
- Paintable blanket ("Maldecke" / "Decke zum Bemalen") that children and families colour in with textile pens.

## Confirmed facts (the ONLY facts the agent may state)
- ⚠️ Not yet provided: material, size, care / washing / ironing temperature, what is in the set (pens?), price, shipping.
  → Until filled in, every product-specific statement must be marked `⚠️ PRÜFEN` in drafts.

## Never
- No medical or developmental claims (e.g. "fördert nachweislich …").
- No unconfirmed specs ("hidden spec rule") and no results that were not tested ("proof gate").
- No numbers from the old research library (written for another brand, contains invented figures).
